## How to sign

Contributions are accepted only from contributors who have agreed to this CLA.

- **Individuals:** The pull-request workflow records your agreement. By opening a
  pull request to this repository, you certify that you have read and agree to
  this CLA and to the Developer Certificate of Origin (see `CONTRIBUTING.md`),
  and you add a `Signed-off-by: Your Name <you@example.com>` line (via
  `git commit -s`) to each commit. A CLA-assistant check may also ask you to
  click to agree on your first pull request.
- **Entities / employers:** If you are contributing on behalf of a company,
  please contact the Maintainer to arrange a Corporate CLA before submitting.
